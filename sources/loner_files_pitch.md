Loner Files are complete solo scenarios for the Loner RPG: self-contained, ready to play, and built around a single loaded situation.

Each File drops you into a world mid-breath: a cast of characters with agendas and secrets, a location under pressure, a clock already running. The oracle and your instincts determine what happens. When the fiction reaches certain moments, a confrontation, a revelation, a point of no return, something authored and specific is waiting there.

Not a gamebook. Not a sandbox. A situation that was already in motion before you arrived.

Pick up a File, read the briefing, and find out how it ends.
