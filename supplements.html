Geared Towards Loner

The Geared Towards Loner line is a growing family of standalone games built on the same minimalist engine as Loner. Each title uses the familiar core (tags and the Oracle) but adds its own flavor, tools, and focus to bring out the essence of a genre.

Every book is complete in itself: you can pick it up and play without owning the Loner core rules. At the same time, all titles are interoperable: supplements, Adventure Packs, and guides can be mixed freely across the line.

A Shared Philosophy

Minimalist Rules No new dice, no stat crunch, just tags, fiction, and the Oracle.
Genre Resonance Each title highlights what makes its genre sing, from pulp cliffhangers to temporal paradoxes.
Interoperability All titles can draw on the same supplements, Adventure Packs, and community content.
